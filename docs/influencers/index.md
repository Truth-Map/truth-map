High-reach online voices shape what large audiences believe about COVID origins, vaccines, intelligence, elections, free speech, and institutional trust. Stance changes, funding models, and the ratio of primary-sourced claims versus pure opinion are material signals. This area treats those signals as first-class data.

This is the modern parallel to the historical intelligence–media nexus. It is **not** a popularity ranking or membership list.

---

## How we look at influencers

Every tracked voice is examined on the same axes:

| Axis | What we record |
|------|----------------|
| **Stance Timeline** | Documented positions over time on the standardized topics. Before → after, with dates and sources. |
| **Funding & Monetization** | Publicly knowable model (platform deals, subscriptions, sponsors, institutional contracts). Opaque claims stay Contested until primary evidence appears. |
| **Claim Style** | Density of primary-linked claims versus high-volume opinion / narrative. |
| **Institutional History** | Documented exits, platform conflicts, or continued institutional affiliation. |
| **Pattern / Cluster** | Observable grouping with other voices. Association is recorded as association only. |

Causal claims about *why* a stance changed start as **Contested** unless strong primary evidence of the exchange or directive exists.

## Grading legend (simple)

We do **not** give people a single “score.” We surface a few readable signals so visitors can scan patterns.

| Signal | What it means |
|--------|---------------|
| **Institutional Exit** | Documented break from a major platform or employer that itself became a public signal |
| **Stance Shift** | Clear before → after change on a standardized topic with primary sources |
| **Funding Transparency** | How much of the monetization model is publicly knowable (Partial / Improved / Opaque) |
| **Claim Style** | Rough density of primary-linked claims vs high-volume opinion/narrative (descriptive only) |
| **Cluster** | Observable grouping already identified in the research module |

## Rules that never relax here

1. Primary sources first (timestamped statements, transcripts, filings, verified sponsorships).  
2. Association ≠ control, payment, or coordination.  
3. Stance *changes* are high-value; causal explanations about the change are labeled separately.  
4. Contested stays Contested until primary documents move it.  
5. No membership dumps. Inclusion requires clear documented relevance to tracked topics or a material funding/stance-shift signal.  
6. Open residuals stay visible with status labels.
